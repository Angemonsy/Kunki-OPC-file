# Frontend Slides — Style Presets

Curated visual styles for Frontend Slides. Each preset is inspired by real design references — no generic "AI slop" aesthetics. Abstract shapes only — no illustrations/clipart.

## Dark Themes

### 1. Bold Signal
- **Vibe**: Confident, modern, high impact
- **Colors**: Dark blue-gray gradient background, bright orange accent card, white text
- **Fonts**: Archivo Black (display) / Space Grotesk (body)
- **Best for**: Pitches, keynote openings, startup decks

---

### 2. Electric Studio
- **Vibe**: Clean, professional, high contrast
- **Colors**: Split background — left side pure white, right side deep electric blue
- **Fonts**: Manrope (both display and body)
- **Best for**: Professional presentations, agency work, case studies

---

### 3. Creative Voltage
- **Vibe**: Energetic, retro-modern, eye-catching
- **Colors**: Split background — electric blue + neon yellow
- **Fonts**: Syne (display) / Space Mono (monospace body)
- **Best for**: Creative portfolios, design talks, creative agencies

---

### 4. Dark Botanical
- **Vibe**: Elegant, sophisticated, premium
- **Colors**: Very dark warm gray background, soft warm organic abstract shapes, off-white text
- **Fonts**: Cormorant (display) / IBM Plex Sans (body)
- **Best for**: Luxury brands, think pieces, academic talks

---

## Light Themes

### 5. Notebook Tabs
- **Vibe**: Editorial, organized, notebook/academic
- **Colors**: Cream paper background, colored vertical side tabs for each section
- **Fonts**: Bodoni Moda (display) / DM Sans (body)
- **Best for**: Lecture notes, documentation, educational content

---

### 6. Pastel Geometry
- **Vibe**: Friendly, approachable, modern minimal
- **Colors**: Off-white background with vertical "pill" shapes in soft pastel hues
- **Fonts**: Plus Jakarta Sans
- **Best for**: Product launches, startup team introductions, friendly talks

---

### 7. Split Pastel
- **Vibe**: Playful, modern, two-tone
- **Colors**: Vertical split background in two complementary pastel colors
- **Fonts**: Outfit
- **Best for**: Creative talks, design portfolios, personal brand presentations

---

### 8. Vintage Editorial
- **Vibe**: Witty, personality-driven, classic editorial
- **Colors**: Warm cream background, geometric muted accent shapes, dark gray text
- **Fonts**: Fraunces (display) / Work Sans (body)
- **Best for**: Opinion essays, think pieces, personal talks

---

## Specialty Themes

### 9. Neon Cyber
- **Vibe**: Futuristic, techy, moody
- **Colors**: Deep navy background with neon glow accents, subtle floating particles
- **Fonts**: Clash Display (display) / Satoshi (body)
- **Best for**: AI talks, tech demos, futurism topics

---

### 10. Terminal Green
- **Vibe**: Developer-focused, hacker aesthetic, code-like
- **Colors**: GitHub dark background with classic green text like old terminal
- **Fonts**: JetBrains Mono (monospace for everything)
- **Best for**: Tech talks, engineering demos, developer tools

---

### 11. Swiss Modern
- **Vibe**: Minimal, clean, precise, Bauhaus-inspired
- **Colors**: Strict black + white with one bold red accent
- **Fonts**: Archivo (display) / Nunito (body)
- **Best for**: Design theory, academic talks, minimal portfolios

---

### 12. Paper & Ink
- **Vibe**: Literary, editorial, typography-focused
- **Colors**: Warm cream paper background, dark gray text, crimson accents
- **Fonts**: Cormorant Garamond (display) / Source Serif 4 (body)
- **Best for**: Book talks, literary essays, think pieces, interviews
