### Unit 1 Section 3 重点短语（翻译常用）

**已经在"完形填空"部分列出，这里补充一些句子翻译示例：**

> 中国GDP在2020年同比增长2.3%，达到101.6万亿元人民币（约15.7万亿美元），跨越了百万亿元门槛。一季度同比收缩6.8%，二季度增长3.2%，三季度增长4.9%，四季度反弹6.5%，V型反弹让中国成为唯一实现正增长的主要经济体。
>
> **Translation:**
> *China's GDP grew by 2.3 percent year on year to hit 101.6 trillion yuan (about 15.7 trillion U.S. dollars) in 2020, surpassing the 100 trillion yuan threshold. From a 6.8-percent contraction in Q1, to 3.2-percent expansion in Q2, 4.9-percent recovery in Q3 and to a 6.5-percent rebound in Q4, the V-shaped trajectory made China the only major economy to log positive growth.*

| 请给我们报...价                | Please offer … / make an offer for …                                                         |
| 我们想询问...详情               | We'd like to enquire about / make an enquiry for / know / have details of …                  |
| 贵方能告诉我们...供货条件/供货数量吗     | Please let us know on what terms / what quantities you can supply …                          |
| 能否寄给我们...报价单/价目表/最新目录/样品 | Would (Could) you please send us your quotation (price list, latest catalogue, sample) for … |


**实盘 (Firm offer):** 有明确具体条款（至少品名、数量、价格）+ 有效期

```
We offer subject to your reply reaching here,

make you the following offer,

The offer is/remains valid/firm/open/effective for 20 days / until June 30.
```

有效期表达：

- within two weeks/five days. 两周/五天内
- before June 30, 2026. 2026年6月30日前
- not later than June 30, 2026. 不迟于2026年6月30日
- by June 30, 2026. 到2026年6月30日截止

**虚盘 (Non-firm offer):** 无有效期

```
We offer subject to

We make the following offer without engagement/obligation.
This offer is without engagement,
```

常见条件：

- our final confirmation. 以我方最后确认为准
- change without notice. 随时调整，不另行通知
- materials being available. 以原材料供应为准
- goods being unsold. 以货物未售出为准
- prior sale. 以先售为准

| ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 现报1000打毛巾(towel)如下，以我方最后确认为准。         | We make the following offer for 1000 dozen towels subject to our final confirmation.                                                                                      |
| 为复你方5月10日的询盘，我方特向你方发盘如下，以你方在7天内接受为有效。 | In reply to your enquiry of May 10 / As requested by you on May 10, we offer as follows / we are offering you the following subject to your acceptance within seven days. |

#### 保险

关键术语：

- insurer; underwriter 保险人/承保人
- the insured 被保险人
- insurance broker 保险经纪人
- insurance amount 保险金额
- insurance coverage/cover 保险险别
- insurance premium 保险费
- insurance policy; insurance certificate 保险单/保险凭证
- total loss 全损
- partial loss 部分损失
  - general average 共同海损
  - particular average 单独海损

中国保险条款 (CIC):

- **FPA (Free From Particular Average)** 平安险：主要承保全损和共同海损
- **WA/WPA (With Average/With Particular Average)** 水渍险：平安险 + 自然灾害造成的单独海损

保险条款常用表达：

- FOB/CFR/FCA/CPT 术语下：**"Insurance: To be covered by the buyer."** 买方投保

### 范文示例（李悦老师课本原例）

**(Ex.4, p.43) 图表描述范文：**

This graph compares the production and consumption of oil from 1980 to 2016. 

From the graph we can see that the production stood at 60 million barrels per day in 1980. In the next four years it fell to 50 million barrels in 1984. However, by 1986 it had returned to 60 million barrels. From that point oil production rose steadily for the next 30 years to 80 million barrels in 2016, with only one deviation from this trend, between 2010 and 2014. 

Oil consumption followed a similar pattern over the same period, starting at 60 million barrels in 1980, but then falling and remaining 5-10 million barrels below production from 1986 to 1996. Apart from slight dips in consumption in 2000 and 2006, production and consumption remained at a similar level until 2010, when consumption started to exceed production. 

In conclusion, this information demonstrates that oil production has been able to keep pace with demand until recently.
