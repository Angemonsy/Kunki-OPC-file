## 二、正式报告标准结构（三段式模板）

```
1. Introduction / Introduction（开头段）
   - 一句话介绍图表：这张图比较了什么时间段内什么数据

2. Main Body（主体段）

   - 用连接词衔接

   - 总结整体趋势，给出简单结论
```

## 三、各类图表描述常用表达（上升/下降/波动/平稳）

### 1️⃣ 描述"上升"的词汇

| 词性 | 词汇 | 用法示例 |
|------|------|----------|
| 动词 | rose / grew / increased | Production rose from 50 to 80 million barrels. |
| 动词 | climbed / gained | The GDP climbed by 2.3 percent year on year. |
| 动词 | increased steadily / rose steadily | Oil production increased steadily over 30 years. |
| 名词 | a rise / an increase | There was a steady rise in production. |
| 形容词 | growing / increasing | The increasing demand for oil. |

### 2️⃣ 描述"下降"的词汇

| 词性 | 词汇 | 用法示例 |
|------|------|----------|
| 动词 | fell / dropped / declined | Production fell to 50 million barrels. |
| 动词 | decreased / dipped | Consumption decreased slightly in 2000. |
| 名词 | a fall / a drop / a decline | There was a slight drop in consumption. |
| 形容词 | falling / declining | Declining oil production. |

### 3️⃣ 描述"保持平稳/波动"

| 情况 | 常用表达 |
|------|----------|
| 保持平稳 | remained stable / stayed at the same level / remained constant |
| 保持在...以下 | remained 5-10 million barrels below production |
| 保持相似水平 | remained at a similar level |
| 有波动 | fluctuated / there were fluctuations |
| 低于... | ... stayed below ... |
| 超过... | ... started to exceed ... |

### 4️⃣ 描述数据对比

- compared with... → 和...相比
- compared to... → 和...相比
- as can be seen from the graph → 从图中可以看出
- from the graph we can see that → 从图中我们可以看出
- over the period from... to... → 在...到...这段时间里
- with only one deviation from this trend → 只有一次偏离这个趋势

## 五、完整满分范文（课本原题）

**题目：** This graph compares the production and consumption of oil from 1980 to 2016.

**范文：**

```
This graph compares the production and consumption of oil from 1980 to 2016. 

From the graph we can see that the production stood at 60 million barrels per day in 1980. In the next four years it fell to 50 million barrels in 1984. However, by 1986 it had returned to 60 million barrels. From that point oil production rose steadily for the next 30 years to 80 million barrels in 2016, with only one deviation from this trend, between 2010 and 2014. 

Oil consumption followed a similar pattern over the same period, starting at 60 million barrels in 1980, but then falling and remaining 5-10 million barrels below production from 1986 to 1996. Apart from slight dips in consumption in 2000 and 2006, production and consumption remained at a similar level until 2010, when consumption started to exceed production. 

In conclusion, this information demonstrates that oil production has been able to keep pace with demand until recently.
```

## 八、突击写作得分要点

1. **必须分段**：至少分3段（开头+主体+结论），不管字多字少，一定分段
2. **多用本课学过的经贸词汇**：就是上面表格里这些，写上就加分
3. **趋势描述准确**：上升就用rose/increased，下降就用fell/declined，不用换太偏的词
4. **时间时态对**：描述过去到现在的数据，用过去时和现在完成时
5. **结尾总结**：一句话总结整体趋势就行，不用写太复杂
