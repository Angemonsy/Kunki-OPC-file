## Unit 1 Section 3 - China's economy 可能考点

### GDP相关术语

| 术语（英文） | 中文翻译 | 可能的描述 |
|--------------|----------|------------|
| **GDP (Gross Domestic Product)** | 国内生产总值 | The total value of goods and services produced within a country in a specific period. |
| **nominal GDP** | 名义GDP | GDP calculated at current market prices without adjusting for inflation. |
| **real GDP** | 实际GDP | GDP adjusted for inflation to account for price changes. |
| **GDP per capita** | 人均GDP | Total GDP divided by the population. |
| **per capita disposable income** | 人均可支配收入 | Income available to people after tax, available for spending or saving. |
| **CPI (consumer price index)** | 居民消费价格指数 | Measures changes in the price level of consumer goods and services. |
| **PPI (producer price index)** | 工业生产者出厂价格指数 | Measures the average change in selling prices received by domestic producers for their output. |

## Unit 2 Section 1 - Types of international trade 可能考点（重点！考得最多）

### 国际贸易基本类型

| 术语（英文） | 中文翻译 | 可能的描述 |
|--------------|----------|------------|
| **export** | 出口 | Selling goods or services produced in one country to buyers in another country. |
| **import** | 进口 | Bringing goods or services produced in another country into one's own country for sale or use. |
| **transit trade** | 转口贸易 | Goods are transported from one country to another through a third country, without changing ownership. |
| **entrepot trade** | 保税仓库贸易/转口贸易 | Importing goods into a bonded area, adding value, then re-exporting without import duties. |
| **bilateral trade** | 双边贸易 | Trade between two countries under negotiated agreements. |
| **multilateral trade** | 多边贸易 | Trade among multiple countries under international agreements. |

### 商品名称辨析（容易考）

| 术语（英文） | 中文翻译 | 可能的描述 |
|--------------|----------|------------|
| **goods** | 商品 | A general term for tangible items at all stages of production. |
| **merchandise** | 商品 | Tangible goods for consumers, commonly used in retail. |
| **commodity** | 大宗商品 | Raw materials or agricultural products such as gold, oil, wheat. |
| **product** | 产品 | Anything produced by a process, includes tangible and intangible goods. |
| **cargo** | 货物 | Goods being transported by ship, plane or other large transport. |
| **freight** | 货物 | Goods transported by train, truck or ship; also the cost of transporting goods. |

### 其他贸易类型

| 术语（英文） | 中文翻译 | 可能的描述 |
|--------------|----------|------------|
| **processing trade** | 加工贸易 | Importing raw materials, processing them in China, then exporting finished products. |
| **compensation trade** | 补偿贸易 | Foreign supplier provides machinery/technology, payment is made with produced goods over time. |
| **countertrade** | 对等贸易 | International trade where goods are exchanged for other goods, not for hard currency. |
| **intra-industry trade** | 产业内贸易 | Two countries exchange similar goods produced within the same industry. |
| **inter-industry trade** | 产业间贸易 | Trade between different industries: one country exports goods from one industry, imports from another. |

### 贸易壁垒与WTO

| 术语（英文） | 中文翻译 | 可能的描述 |
|--------------|----------|------------|
| **tariff** | 关税 | A tax imposed on imported goods. |
| **Most-favoured-nation (MFN)** | 最惠国待遇 | Treat all WTO members equally - if you give a favor to one, you give it to all. |
| **National treatment** | 国民待遇 | Treat foreign goods and services the same as domestic ones once they enter the market. |

### 🔥 区域经济一体化（高频考点！就是你说的这种题）

| 术语（英文） | 中文翻译 | 可能的描述 |
|--------------|----------|------------|
| **regional economic integration** | 区域经济一体化 | When two or more countries cooperate to reduce or eliminate trade restrictions, creating a shared market. |
| **Free trade area** | 自由贸易区 | _________________________________ | Goods and services move freely among member countries. |
| **Customs union** | 关税同盟 | Free trade area + **common external tariff** against non-members. |
| **Common market** | 共同市场 | Customs union + **free movement of factors of production** (labor, capital). |
| **Economic union** | 经济联盟 | Common market + **common economic policies** (monetary, fiscal, etc). |

## 🔍 最可能考的几道题预测

1. ```
   _______________ is when two or more countries cooperate to reduce or eliminate trade restrictions, creating a shared market.
   答案：regional economic integration
   ```

2. ```
   A _______________ is a customs union with free movement of labor and capital.
   答案：common market
   ```

3. ```
   When you import goods into a bonded area, add value, then export them without paying import duties, this is called _______________.
   答案：entrepot trade
   ```

4. ```
   The total value of goods and services produced within a country is called _______________.
   答案：GDP (Gross Domestic Product)
   ```

5. ```
   _______________ measures changes in the prices of consumer goods and services.
   答案：CPI (consumer price index)
   ```
