## Unit 1 Section 1: Vocabulary - Economic issues

### 核心词汇

**economy [ɪˈkɒnəmɪ] n.**
1. [C] the system by which the trade, industry and money of a country or region are organized
   - a market/planned/industrial economy
2. [C] the wealth and resources of a country or region, esp. in terms of the production and consumption of goods and services
   - The Chinese economy grows at an annual rate of 5 percent.
   - The economy is beginning to revive.
3. [C] a country considered as an economic system
   - one of the fastest-growing economies in Asia-Pacific region
4. [U, C] the careful use of money, time, etc. so that very little is wasted
   - They've had to make economies since he lost his job.
   - There are great improvements in the fuel economy of cars.
5. (*attrib 做定语*) inexpensive to buy or use
   - economy class [U]
   - an economy pack/size
   - an economy car

**economic [ˌiːkəˈnɒmɪk] adj.**
1. [only before noun] connected with the trade, industry and development of a country, region or society.
   - economic reform/growth/policy/benefit
2. making a profit, or likely to make a profit 盈利的, 有利可图的 ≈ profitable

**economically [ˌiːkəˈnɒmɪkli] adv.** - 经济地，在经济上

**economical [ˌiːkəˈnɒmɪkəl] adj.**
- not spending or costing much money; not wasteful 经济实惠的，节俭的，节约的
  - Electric vehicles are clean, quiet, and economical.
  - Buying in bulk is more economical than shopping for small quantities.

**economics [ˌiːkəˈnɒmɪks] [U]**
1. (a) the study of how a society organizes its money, trade and industry;
   (b) economic theory, principles, or practices

**economist [ɪˈkɒnəmɪst] [C]**
- a person who studies, teaches, or writes about economics

**economize/economise [ɪˈkɒnəˌmaɪz] vi.**
- to make an effort to save money, time, resources, etc.
  - We must economize on water.
  - A lot of companies are trying to economize by not taking on new staff.

- Growth trend
- Peak

- Trough

- **Boom**: extreme expansion
- **Recession**: A significant decline in economic activity spread across the economy, lasting more than a few months (two consecutive quarters), normally visible in GDP, income, employment, industrial production, and wholesale-retail sales.
- **Depression**: A sustained, long-term downturn in the economy that lasts three or more years or results in a decline in GDP of at least 10%. It follows the second stage of recession if the period of economic decline is prolonged and severe.

## Unit 2 Section 1: Types of international trade

### Types of International Trade - Vocabulary

1. **export**: the act of selling goods or services produced domestically to foreign markets
   - **import**: the act of bringing goods or services into a country from abroad for domestic sale or use
   - export [U, C, vt.] /ˈekspɔːt ɪkˈspɔːt;/ → exporter / ekˈspɔːtə(r)/
   - import [U, C, vt.] / ˈɪmpɔːt ɪmˈpɔːt/ → importer / ɪmˈpɔːtə(r)/

2. **transit trade**: the cross-border trade in which goods pass through a third country to their final destination, but ownership of the goods does not pass to the third country

3. **entrepot trade**: the practice of importing goods from one country and re-exporting them to another country after adding some value, with or without undergoing additional processing and with no import duties imposed
   - entrepot: / ˈɒntrəpəʊ / - also called a transshipment port, is a trading post, port, city, or warehouse used for the import, storage, and subsequent re-export of goods with no customs duties imposed
   - bonded area/zone/warehouse

4. **bilateral trade**: the exchange of goods and services between two nations through negotiated agreements
   - **multilateral trade**: the trading of goods and services among several countries, often under the auspices of an international agreement

5. **visible trade/trade in goods/goods trade/merchandise trade**: the exchange of physical goods in every stage of production between countries
   - **invisible trade/trade in services**: the international exchange of physically intangible items such as services, income from foreign investment, intellectual property and patents

### Noun distinctions

- **goods** [n. plural]: (a broad term) tangible items in various stages of production, including raw materials, finished products, and commodities
- **merchandise** [U]: tangible items that are available for purchase by consumers, typically finished products and consumer goods, more commonly used in the retail industry
- **commodity** [C]: a raw material or primary agricultural product, such as gold, oil, wheat, grain, coffee, etc.
- **product** [C]: items that are created, produced or manufactured, as a result of a specific process or activity, including both tangible and intangible items such as physical goods, services, software, or intellectual property, etc.
- **cargo** [C, U]: goods that are being transported by ships, aircrafts, or other large vehicles
  - cargo plane, cargo ship (cf. freight plane, freight ship)
- **freight** [U]: goods that are being transported by trains, trucks or ships; also the payment or money charged for transporting goods

### More Trade Types

6. **processing trade**: a type of trade in which domestic firms import raw materials, auxiliary materials, parts and components, accessories, and packaging materials from abroad, and re-export finished products after processing or assembling these materials or parts
   - processing with imported materials (import-and-assembly)
   - processing with supplied materials (pure assembly)

7. **compensation trade**: a form of trade in which the supplier (exporter) of machinery, technology, manufacturing facilities or raw materials to a foreign enterprise agrees to be paid in the form of part of that enterprise's output over an agreed-on period

8. **countertrade**: a reciprocal form of international trade in which goods or services are exchanged for other goods or services rather than for hard currency

9. **intra-industry trade**: a type of trade in which two countries exchange goods that are similar to those produced in the other country - the exchange of similar goods within the same industry between countries

10. **inter-industry trade**: a type of trade in which a country exports goods from a completely different sector than those it imports from its trading partner - the exchange of goods from different industries between countries

### Trade Barriers

- **tariff** [C] = duty [C] / customs duty = customs [plural]
  - to impose/introduce/levy/put ~ on (goods)
  - to pay ~
  - to avoid/escape/evade ~
  - to cut ~
  - to eliminate/remove ~

### WTO Principles

**Most-favoured-nation (MFN):** treating other people equally
- Grant someone a special favour (such as a lower customs duty rate for one of their products) and you have to do the same for all other WTO members.

**National treatment:** treating foreigners and locals equally
- Imported and locally-produced goods should be treated equally — at least after the foreign goods have entered the market. The same should apply to foreign and domestic services, and to foreign and local trademarks, copyrights and patents.

**Key WTO features:**
- The multilateral trading system is an attempt by governments to make the business environment stable and predictable.
- In the WTO, when countries agree to open their markets for goods or services, they "bind" their commitments.
- Another is to make countries' trade rules as clear and public ("transparent") as possible.
- The system does allow tariffs and, in limited circumstances, other forms of protection. More accurately, it is a system of rules dedicated to open, fair and undistorted competition.

### Trade Disputes
- trade conflicts/disputes/tensions/wars
- dispute settlement mechanism
- to file a WTO complaint against/over ...
- to file a complaint with the WTO against/over ...
- to complain to the WTO against/over ...

### Regional Economic Integration

- **regional economic integration**: the collaboration of two or more countries to lower or remove trade restrictions and create a shared market and business opportunities for one another
- **Free trade area**: free movement of goods & services
- **Customs union**: free trade area + common external tariff
- **Common market**: customs union + free mobility of production factors
- **Economic union**: common market + common economic policies

### China's Foreign Trade

- export diversification (出口多元化)
- to keep the volume of foreign trade stable and refine its mix (外贸结构)
- to guide enterprises in optimizing resource allocation on the global market (引导企业优化全球市场布局)
- to facilitate the growth of the "cross-border e-commerce plus overseas warehouses" model (跨境电商加海外仓模式)
- to promote the development of logistics services for international delivery (国际寄递物流体系建设)
- to expand trade in intermediate goods, advance digital trade and green trade, and boost border trade (拓展中间品贸易，发展数字贸易、绿色贸易，提升边境贸易)
