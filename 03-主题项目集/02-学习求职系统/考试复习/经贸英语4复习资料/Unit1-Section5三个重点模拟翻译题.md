## 🔹 重点一：Income（收入分配）

**请将下列中文句子翻译成英文：**

1. 当前，城乡和区域之间收入分配仍然存在较大差距。

<details>
<summary>点击查看参考答案</summary>

```
There are still wide gaps in income distribution between urban and rural areas and between regions.
```
</details>

2. 我们应该努力保持居民收入增长与经济增长同步，劳动报酬提高与劳动生产率提高同步。

<details>
<summary>点击查看参考答案</summary>

```
Personal incomes should increase in step with economic growth, and remuneration should rise in tandem with labor productivity increases.
```
</details>

3. 我们需要构建初次分配、再分配、第三次分配协调配套的制度体系。

<details>
<summary>点击查看参考答案</summary>

```
We need to build an institutional framework under which primary, secondary, and tertiary distribution are well coordinated and mutually complementary.
```
</details>

4. 要多渠道增加城乡居民财产性收入，同时通过税收、社会保障、转移支付加强再分配调节。

<details>
<summary>点击查看参考答案</summary>

```
We should leverage multiple channels to increase the property income of both urban and rural residents, and promote further redistribution by means of taxation, social security, and transfer payments.
```
</details>

5. 我们要扩大中等收入群体，努力形成橄榄型分配格局。

<details>
<summary>点击查看参考答案</summary>

```
We need to enlarge the middle-income group and work to form an oval-shaped structure of distribution.
```
</details>

6. 我们需要实施收入增长计划，激发下沉市场消费活力，释放旅游文化等领域消费潜力。

<details>
<summary>点击查看参考答案</summary>

```
We need to implement an income growth plan, energize consumption off-line and in lower-tier markets, and unlock spending potential in areas such as culture and tourism.
```
</details>

Data has already become an important factor of production.

<details>

Transformation and upgrading of traditional industries continues, and emerging industries and industries of the future have achieved impressive growth.
