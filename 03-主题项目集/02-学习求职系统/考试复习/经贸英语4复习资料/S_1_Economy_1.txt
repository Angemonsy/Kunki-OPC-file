=== Slide 2 ===
Vocabulary
economy [ɪˈkɒnəmɪ]: n. 
1. [C] the system by which the trade, industry and money of a country or region are organized
     - a market/planned/industrial economy
2. [C] the wealth and resources of a country or region, esp. in terms of the production and consumption of goods and services
     - The Chinese economy grows at an annual rate of 5 percent.
     - The economy is beginning to revive.

=== Slide 3 ===
Vocabulary
economy [ɪˈkɒnəmɪ]: n.
3. [C] a country considered as an economic system
    - one of the fastest-growing economies in Asia-Pacific region
4. [U, C] the careful use of money, time, etc. so that very little is wasted
    - They’ve had to make economies since he lost his job.
    - There are great improvements in the fuel economy of cars.

=== Slide 5 ===
Vocabulary
economic [ˌiːkəˈnɒmɪk]: adj.
1. [only before noun] connected with the trade, industry and development of a country, region or society.
     - economic reform/growth/policy/benefit
2. making a profit, or likely to make a profit 盈利的, 有利可图的
     ≈ profitable
 economically [ˌiːkəˈnɒmɪkli]: adv. 
     经济地，在经济上

=== Slide 6 ===
Vocabulary
economical [ˌiːkəˈnɒmɪkəl]: adj.
     not spending or costing much money; not wasteful 
       经济实惠的，节俭的，节约的
  - Electric vehicles are clean, quiet, and economical.
  - Buying in bulk is more economical than shopping for small quantities.
 economically: adv.
     经济地，节俭地，节约地

=== Slide 12 ===
rate: a measurement of the number of times something happens in a particular period
    e.g. birth/crime/success rate
            interest/exchange/mortgage rate
ratio: the relationship between two amounts, represented by two numbers or a percentage, expressing how much bigger one is than the other
    e.g. The ratio of teachers to students is 1:20.
           teacher-student ratio; male-female ratio
Vocabulary

=== Slide 13 ===
benefit [C or U]: money provided by the government to people who need financial help because they are ill, unemployed, etc.
   e.g. unemployment/disability benefit
subsidy [C]: money given by a government or an organization to reduce the cost of producing a product, etc. and to help to keep prices low
   e.g. export/agricultural subsidy
    subsidize: vt.
Vocabulary
