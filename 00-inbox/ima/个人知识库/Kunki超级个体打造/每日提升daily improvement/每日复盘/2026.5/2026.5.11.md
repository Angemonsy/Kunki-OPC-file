四、人际情绪与自我内耗

处在迷茫折磨期、百废待兴的阶段：大方向坚定走科技AI赛道、往港科广和商业化深耕，但具体落地小路径、时间分配、雅思推进、项目取舍都很迷茫，只能学着和自卑、孤独、阶段性焦虑共处。

六、当日小结
&\#x20;
今天是平平无奇的过渡一天，事务都在按部就班推进，没有突破也没有荒废。
最大的收获不在做事，而在情绪复盘：看清自己功利主义带来的社交代价、接受圈层迭代的孤独、承认和大佬之间的差距，同时守住大方向不动摇。接下来要减少无意义消遣，把被杂事占用的时间，重新收拢到自我提升、雅思备考和核心AI成长上，减少内耗，踏实往前走。

2. Project Reflection & Inner Struggle
I completed and delivered the sample draft of the AI book project today, yet I felt quite tangled and depressed. The industry iterates extremely fast. By the time the book is officially published, the related models and ecology may have changed completely, making the content easy to be outdated.
&\#x20;
I took this project on impulse before, and now it’s hard to quit. I lack genuine interest in this project, and it occupies a lot of time that should have been used for self-input and AI improvement. I cannot clearly confirm its long-term value to me, so I can only keep promoting it as scheduled.

3. Campus Observation & Social Regret
Watching graduation atmosphere on campus, I felt envious of those who have close friends and community honor recognition. I deeply regretted being overly utilitarian in the first two years of college, focusing only on academic performance while ignoring clubs and offline social life.
&\#x20;
The cost of excessive academic pursuit is a narrow interpersonal circle and almost no hobby-based social connection. Now I am quite transparent on campus with few deep relationships. If I want to develop campus influence in the future, I can only rely on online social platforms like Xiaohongshu. I truly realize you have to pay a price for every choice. My outstanding grades came at the cost of social life and emotional support, leaving me isolated and having no one to share pressure with.
&\#x20;
4. Emotional Reflection & Loneliness
Some old friends gradually drifted apart. I occasionally reflect on whether I have personal problems in interpersonal communication. I am always used to providing value to others, but I have never become a reliable emotional harbor for others, which often brings me lonely and emotional moments.
&\#x20;
After entering the AI circle, I have seen many outstanding seniors and experts, making me feel small and powerless. My life track and cognition have far separated from my old social circle. I cannot force old friends to keep up with my pace and have to accept the natural iteration of social connections.
&\#x20;
Now I am in a confused and struggling period. My general direction in technology and AI remains firm, but I feel lost in specific implementation paths, time arrangement and project choices. I am learning to coexist with inferiority, loneliness and periodic anxiety.
&\#x20;
5. Life Trivia & Summary
I kept daily light plain makeup training to maintain personal image management. The planned photo shoot with a senior was missed due to my morning workout schedule, and I remained calm about it, only following up on invoice matters afterward.
&\#x20;
Overall, today was an ordinary transitional day with routine tasks steadily moving forward. The biggest gain is emotional self-reflection: recognizing the social cost of utilitarianism, accepting the loneliness of circle iteration, and acknowledging the gap with industry seniors. From now on, I need to reduce meaningless leisure, recover fragmented time for self-improvement, IELTS preparation and AI growth, and move forward steadily with less internal consumption.
