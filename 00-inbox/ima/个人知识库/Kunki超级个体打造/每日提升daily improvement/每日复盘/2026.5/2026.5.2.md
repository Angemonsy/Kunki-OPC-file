核心状态
 
5月2日周六整体过得平淡又内耗，作息松散、白天状态低迷，线下活动看清自己AI能力已到L6，但严重缺变现体系和清晰路径；会员代充项目遇渠道风控、售后棘手、小幅赔本，陷入不上不下的中间尴尬期。同时陷入雄竞、情感、人际关系多重内耗，看清很多人脉只是表面利益关系、自己常被当工具人消耗；被“累是伪命题”点醒，明白心累本质是阶段性失败、没拿到结果。生活端还有学业演讲作业未准备、外公身体抱恙的突发心事，情绪杂糅、感慨世事无常，一边迷茫焦虑，一边逼着自己沉淀体系、找准角色、继续往前走。
 
一、日间日常作息
 
- 早上起得很晚，作息散漫，起床喝热牛奶，用iPad处理零碎事情、打游戏消磨时间。
- 起床状态很差，一整个上午基本没推进正经事，简单吃饭、虚度时光。
- 午后调整状态，下午2点半到6点全程参加广州OPC+夜校线下分享活动，聚焦一人公司生态圈搭建。
- 傍晚返程路上和父母视频，得到家人鼓励支持，同时接到外公身体状况不好、时日无多的坏消息，心里沉重、感慨世事无常，打算明天和外婆视频再见一见外公，也安抚父母放平心态。
 
二、AI能力&阶段定位认知
 
- 自测AI能力已达L6级别，去杭州大会前是L5，这段时间认知和实操明显进阶。
- 清醒认知：现在不缺AI技术能力，缺的是成熟变现结构、产品定位、商业闭环。
- 现状尴尬：
比普通小白圈友认知、实操强很多；
但够不着头部大博主、商业高阶玩家，卡在中间不上不下。
- 核心短板：知识、方法论、SOP没有系统化分门别类沉淀，零散不成体系，没法复制交付、规模化变现。
 
三、项目瓶颈&现实受挫
 
- 会员代充项目陷入明显瓶颈，渠道大面积风控，客户需求对接受阻、售后积压。
- 今天处理售后再次小幅赔本，付出精力还亏钱，身心双重疲惫。
- 同行内卷严重，自己看不清精准定位、不知道该往哪个赛道深耕，迷茫感拉满。
 
四、认知破局：累是伪命题
 
- 当下身心俱疲、迷茫躺平，突然想起练咏哥的观点：累是伪命题。
- 真正的累不是身体困，而是心灵妥协、阶段性失败；
做事一直赢、一直拿结果，只有征服感和激情，不会觉得累；
一直输、一直没成果、卡在原地，才会心累、不想坚持。
- 自我点透：现在的疲惫，本质是雄竞失败、项目没结果、变现没打通、成长没突破带来的内耗。
- 接受现实、强迫振作：五一已过半，时间不多，别人的幸福现阶段不属于自己，暂时没有多余窗口去享受，只能先沉下心赶路、拿结果。
 
五、人际&雄竞深度内耗
 
- 感慨落差：原本觉得木讷、在性张力上不如自己的朋友，靠国际数学教培赚到不少钱，还在小红书吸引多位异性暧昧关系。
- 反观自己资源匮乏、情感没有突破口，发自内心觉得挫败、落差感极强。
- 下定决心：把小红书、抖音生活号当成朋友圈曝光，不再主动讨好、不舔别人，用阿尔法思维待人处事，靠平台被看见、被筛选，顺其自然链接同频人脉。
- 看透人脉真相：身边少有真正知心朋友，大多是表面关系、利益往来；
自己太善良、识人不准，经常被人当工具人借用资源、认知和能量，对方成长后转身就走，消耗自己毫无回馈，已经受够这种角色，以后学会设防、不再轻易付出。
 
六、线下活动收获&自我角色定位
 
- 线下林老师分享广州一人公司生态圈、OPC+夜校模式拆解。
- 看清自己当前角色：更偏向乙方、交付方、讲师、学员服务；
内心想做平台方、操盘方，但现阶段实力和积累不够，只能先从乙方交付做起，慢慢沉淀再往平台靠拢。
- 计划明天继续参加自创会线下活动，持续混圈子、吸收模式、找准定位。
 
七、学业&晚间琐事
 
- 晚上明明八点就很困，躺着休息一阵，起来也没高效推进事情。
- 搞定金毛银课程作业，但演讲稿还没背熟、读不顺，后续要准备Presentation课堂演讲，任务积压有点烦躁。
- 晚间继续打磨AI一人公司系统，不知不觉时间耗完，一天平淡落幕，心事繁杂、情绪沉淀。
 
八、当日总结感悟
 
1. 技术已经到位，缺体系、缺产品、缺变现路径，接下来重点是梳理SOP、沉淀方法论、搭建商业结构。
2. 心累不是身体累，是没拿到结果的失败感，接受现状、停止内耗，用行动破局。
3. 放弃主动讨好社交，转型小红书抖音生活号自然曝光，用阿尔法思维立住人设和边界。
4. 看透无效人脉，不再做无偿工具人，学会筛选、设防、专注自身成长。
5. 先安身乙方交付角色，慢慢积累再往平台操盘进阶，不急于求成。
6. 珍惜亲情、看淡无常，稳住心态，把仅剩的时间全部用在搞结果、搞成长上。

Daily Review – May 2nd
 
Core State
 
May 2nd was a plain yet emotionally draining day. I slept late and stayed in a loose daily rhythm, feeling low in the morning. I attended an offline sharing event in the afternoon and clearly realized that my AI ability has reached Level 6, upgraded from Level 6 before the Hangzhou conference. However, I lack a mature monetization system and clear business path.
 
My membership recharge project hit a bottleneck due to channel risk control, resulting in stuck customer demands, after-sales troubles and minor financial losses. I am now in an awkward middle state, far ahead of new beginners but still unable to reach top influencers. I fell into internal friction in social competition and interpersonal relationships, realizing most connections are only interest-based and I have often been used as a tool person. Inspired by a viewpoint, I understood that mental tiredness is essentially the frustration of no tangible results. Meanwhile, I have unfinished speech assignments and sudden family worries about my grandfather’s health, making my mood complicated and heavy.
 
1. Daily Routine & Offline Activity
I got up very late with a poor mental state. I drank hot milk, dealt with trivial things on my iPad and played games, wasting most of the morning without making any real progress.
 
In the afternoon, I joined an offline event about Guangzhou OPC and night school from 2:30 p.m. to 6:00 p.m., focusing on the ecological construction of the one-person company model. On the way back, I video-called my parents and received constant encouragement. Meanwhile, I got the bad news that my grandfather is in poor health and his time may be limited. I plan to video chat with my grandmother to see my grandfather soon and comfort my parents to keep a peaceful mind.
 
2. AI Ability & Self-Positioning
My self-assessed AI capability has risen to L6, a clear improvement compared with L5 before the Hangzhou conference. The key problem now is not technical ability, but the lack of monetization framework, product positioning and closed-loop business logic.
 
I am in an awkward intermediate position: I possess much more cognition and practical skills than ordinary beginners, yet still cannot match top commercial bloggers. My knowledge, methodology and standard SOPs are scattered without systematic arrangement, which restricts my ability of replication, delivery and large-scale monetization.
 
3. Project Dilemma & Frustration
My membership recharge project encountered obvious bottlenecks. Large-scale channel risk control blocked customer demands and piled up after-sales problems. I even suffered small losses while handling trivial after-sales work. The whole industry is highly competitive, and I feel confused about my precise track and future direction.
4. Cognitive Awakening: Tiredness is a false concept
I suddenly realized a profound viewpoint: mental tiredness is not physical exhaustion, but inner compromise and frustration caused by no results. People will feel passionate and a sense of conquest when they keep winning; only continuous failure and stagnation lead to weariness and loss of motivation.
 
I understood my current tiredness comes from failure in social competition, blocked project monetization and lack of breakthroughs. Time is limited during the May holiday. I have to accept that I cannot enjoy others’ happiness for now, and can only focus on self-improvement and pursuing tangible results first.
 
5. Social Internal Friction & Mindset Adjustment
I felt a strong sense of gap when seeing an acquaintance with dull personal charm earn much money through education training and build ambiguous relationships via Xiaohongshu. By contrast, I lack similar social resources and emotional channels, bringing me a strong sense of frustration.
 
I decided to take Xiaohongshu and Douyin life accounts as my social circle for natural exposure, stop taking the initiative to please others, and adopt alpha mindset in interpersonal interactions. I also see through superficial relationships: few true bosom friends exist, most are only interest-driven. I have been kind and inaccurate in judging people, often being used as a tool to provide resources and cognition for others, then abandoned after being drained. I am determined to set boundaries and no longer play such a role.
 
6. Role Orientation & Study Arrangement
After listening to the offline sharing, I clarified my current role: I am more suitable to be the Party B responsible for delivery, lecturing and student service. Although I aspire to be a platform operator, I can only start from Party B and accumulate experience for future upgrading. I plan to attend another offline event tomorrow to expand my vision and positioning.
 
I felt extremely sleepy at 8 p.m. but still delayed my schedule. I finished part of my homework yet still need to memorize and practice for the coming presentation, which brings extra trivial pressure. I spent the rest of the night polishing my one-person AI company system, and the day ended quietly with mixed emotions.

Today is May 2nd, Saturday.
 
I got up really late in the morning and was in a bad mood all the early time. I drank some hot milk, messed around with my iPad, played games and just killed time without doing anything meaningful. After lunch I pulled myself together and went to an offline event from half past two till six o’clock, mainly about Guangzhou OPC and night school, and the whole ecosystem of one-person company.
 
I tested my AI level today, and now I’m at L6. I was only L6 before the Hangzhou conference, so I’ve made clear progress. The funny thing is, I don’t lack AI skills at all right now. What I really lack is a complete monetization structure and a clear path to make money steadily.
 
My membership recharge project is stuck in a big bottleneck. The channels are heavily risk-controlled, so I can’t match customer demands smoothly. I had to deal with a lot of after-sales problems and even lost a little money today. It really wore me out.
 
Right now I’m in an awkward position in the AI circle. I’m way better than those new beginners, but I still can’t reach the level of those top big bloggers and commercial players. The biggest problem is my knowledge and methods are not systematic at all. I need to sort out all my experience, make complete SOPs and settle down my own system as soon as possible.
 
Lately I’ve been trapped in a lot of internal friction. I have a friend who is quite dull and not that charming, but he makes good money from international math education. Surprisingly he has several ambiguous relationships with girls attracted from Xiaohongshu. Compared with him, I feel kind of frustrated and unsuccessful in this aspect.
 
So I made up my mind: I will treat my Xiaohongshu and Douyin life account as my personal moments. I just expose my life naturally, let people notice me and choose to connect with me. I don’t want to take the initiative to please anyone anymore, it’s meaningless. I totally follow the alpha mindset in dealing with people now, even though it also comes with some price.
 
I really want to get solid results soon, but I just don’t have a clear system and roadmap. The industry is super competitive, and I’m confused about my positioning and future direction. I felt really tired and lost today.
 
Then a sentence hit me: being tired is a false concept. People don’t get tired physically, they only get sleepy. If you keep winning all the games and feeling the sense of conquest, you will never feel tired. You only feel tired when you keep losing and failing. That made me realize my exhaustion is basically the frustration of not getting results recently.
 
The May holiday is already half gone. I know others’ happy life is not for me right now. I don’t have the extra time and energy to enjoy that kind of life. I can only keep moving forward quietly and focus on my own growth first.
 
To be honest, I barely have real close friends around me. Most relationships are just superficial and interest-based. I’ve been too nice and bad at judging people. Many people take my resources, my cognition and my energy from me, then leave and run to someone else after getting what they want. I’m really tired of being used as a tool person, and I won’t let it happen again.
 
In the offline event today, I listened to Teacher Lin sharing the one-person company business model. I realized right now I’m more like the Party B, responsible for teaching and delivering value. Deep down I want to be a platform operator, but I know I have to start from being a service provider first and accumulate experience step by step. I plan to join another offline event tomorrow to learn more.
 
I was extremely sleepy at 8 p.m., lay down for a rest but still wasted time after waking up. I finished some school homework, but I still need to memorize and practice my presentation speech, which is quite annoying.
 
On my way back I video-called my parents. They always encourage me a lot, but they also told me some bad news — my grandfather’s health is very poor and his condition is not optimistic. It makes me feel emotional and sigh how unpredictable life is. I plan to video chat with my grandmother tomorrow to see my grandfather, and I also told my parents to keep their mind peaceful.
 
In the evening I kept working on my one-person AI system, and time just passed quickly. The whole day was plain, messy in mood, confused in direction, but also sober enough to know I have to settle down, build my system and chase real results without delay.
