Daily Review
&\#x20;
1. Woke up Early and Went to the Hospital
\- I got up early this morning to go to the hospital for a check-up on my face and skin. Meanwhile, my mom had to go to another hospital for a gastrointestinal check-up, so we went out around 8 o’clock and rushed to the city hospital.
\- I had made an appointment with a dermatologist in advance. He analyzed my facial condition and advised me not to eat high-fat and high-sugar food anymore. He also said that acne is quite common for boys in early adulthood, but my condition was a bit more serious.
\- I received a medicinal facial cleansing and a red light therapy to prevent acne regrowth and reduce inflammation, which cost me some money.
\- I was impressed that hospitals now have very complete procedures and systems with many intelligent systems applied, making the whole experience smooth and convenient.
2. Noon & Afternoon Work
\- My parents picked me up and we went to a supermarket. I had McDonald’s for lunch.
\- In the afternoon, I worked at Starbucks to finish building and optimizing my business-oriented WeChat Moments. It took me a long time; I used Canva and Gemini to polish my copy and create specific pictures.
3. Evening & Future Plans
\- As dinner time approached, I went to the restaurant to meet my high school classmates and close friends. We will spend two days relaxing and having fun together.
\- Most of them study at top universities, and we gather every vacation to reconnect. Originally, I was supposed to go back to my high school this afternoon with them to give sharing sessions to current students, but I made up an excuse to skip it — I told them I had to stay with my sister at the hospital, so I could only meet them for dinner tonight.
\- The truth is I don’t want to go back to my high school or meet the teachers from back then. I went through a lot of difficulties and ups and downs in high school and didn’t have a good time.
\- Still, I appreciate that period because it taught me to become stronger and earn honor and respect through my own efforts — the weak do not deserve respect or status. It pushed me to keep improving myself.
\- Starting from tonight, I will take a break and relax with my friends.
