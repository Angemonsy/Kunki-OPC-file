Daily Review (Feb 20–23: Shanghai & Suzhou Trip)
Core state: Traveled in Shanghai and Suzhou for four days. I was deeply attracted by Shanghai’s urban atmosphere, and confirmed that I will only consider first-tier cities for postgraduate study. I barely promoted my business or social media during the trip. I felt tired and lonely due to long high-speed rail rides and poor internet connection.
&\#x20;
1. Shanghai: Scenic spots & urban experience
• I visited the Oriental Pearl Tower, queued for over 3 hours, but the actual experience was just so-so. I still took some photos as souvenirs.
• I walked along Wukang Road, which was crowded with stylish and attractive people, making me feel the urge to improve my own image.
• I prayed sincerely at City God Temple, wishing for relationship, wealth, academic success and postgraduate recommendation.
• I visited the Bund, Nanjing Road and had dinner at BFC. Overall, Shanghai impressed me deeply with its energetic atmosphere.
• I decided that I will only consider Beijing, Shanghai, Guangzhou and Shenzhen for future development and postgraduate study.
2. Suzhou: Average experience
• I visited Lion Forest Garden and Lingering Garden, but was not interested in the gardens.
• I did not feel much attraction to Suzhou and still prefer first-tier cities.
3. Thoughts on city & postgraduate study
• Guangzhou is most comfortable: low living cost, suitable for starting a business while studying.
• Beijing and Shanghai have higher costs but more top universities and opportunities.
• Shenzhen is moderate but lacks universities; Guangzhou also has limited choices.
• I believe it is unwise to give up top universities in Beijing and Shanghai just for limited resources in the Greater Bay Area.
• My knowledge-based business is not restricted by location, so platform and city matter more.
4. Emotion & state: Loneliness & exhaustion
• I look excellent on paper (ranked first in major), but inside I have few close friends and no relationship.
• I traveled with a hometown friend, but we have little in common due to different majors.
• The round-trip high-speed rail took nearly 10 hours each way, with extremely poor internet, making it impossible to work.
• I did not update social media during the Spring Festival, missing the chance to gain followers.
5. In-depth reflection
• Shanghai broadened my vision and strengthened my determination to pursue top platforms.
• Traveling with family is tiring and restricts freedom; long trips with bad internet seriously reduce efficiency.
• I clearly feel inner loneliness and need to expand social circles gradually.
• I wasted the Spring Festival traffic window by stopping content creation.
• Tomorrow I will return to my normal schedule: English, postgraduate recommendation and business.
