&\#x20;
1. 情绪与心态
&\#x20;

4. 深刻反思
&\#x20;
\- 惰性根深蒂固：空闲时间肆意挥霍，忙碌时又心态浮躁，永远在拖延和临时突击中恶性循环，时间利用率极低。
\- 做事不成体系：长期依靠一时热情，无法沉淀深耕长期事务，没有稳定、规律的执行计划。
\- 自律意识薄弱：自控力不足，摆烂时肆意放纵，醒悟后又极端熬夜内耗，不懂合理分配精力。
&\#x20;
5. 后续改进方向
&\#x20;
\- 戒掉随性摆烂心态，摒弃心血来潮式做事方式，搭建系统化、常态化的日常执行规划。
\- 合理切割碎片化时间，珍惜无课空余时段，拒绝无效休息内耗，提高单位时间利用率。
\- 稳住作息规律，拒绝深夜突击式自我感动，把动力分摊到白天，稳步持续推进各项任务。
\- 沉淀心性，放下浮躁，用实际行动替代口头渴望，培养持之以恒的执行力。

Daily Review – April 16th & 17th
&\#x20;
Core State
&\#x20;
I was in a decadent and slack state for two consecutive days and failed to clock in. Faced with numerous pending tasks, I fell into confusion and self-doubt without clear motivation. My time was severely wasted and fragmented, and I always worked on tasks on a whim rather than in a systematic way.
&\#x20;
I received the application notice of HKUST(GZ) for the 2026 admission and felt confused about the unreasonable arrangement of the university. I started to weigh the priority between domestic postgraduate recommendation and overseas applications. Although I made a sudden breakthrough in AI projects, my daily schedule was chaotic. I clearly realized that I lack the perseverance of real entrepreneurs and urgent changes are needed.
&\#x20;
1. Mood & Mentality
&\#x20;
\- I felt depressed and slack these two days. The more tasks I had, the more confused I became, falling into the same negative state as vacations.
\- I fell into self-denial: I can only stick to things by sudden passion without systematic planning and long-term perseverance.
\- I understand that my desire for success is only verbal. I do not have the determination to burn my boats, and there is a huge gap between me and real entrepreneurs.
&\#x20;
2. Time Management & Daily Routine
&\#x20;
\- On Thursday the 16th, I had no classes, but I wasted the whole day. I rested aimlessly after fitness in the morning and regretted it deeply in the afternoon.
\- On Friday the 17th, my time was split by courses and volunteer activities. I did nothing valuable for my personal plans in the daytime.
\- I lacked self-discipline. I suddenly devoted myself to AI research at 11 p.m. and stayed up until 2 a.m., which affected my offline activities the next day.
&\#x20;
3. Task Progress
&\#x20;
\- Self-media: I edited relevant videos on April 16th and spent plenty of time exploring one-click multi-platform release. The final effect was unsatisfying, and I still need to keep researching better solutions.
\- AI Project: It was stagnant in the early stage and only got sudden inspiration late at night, lacking stable and continuous progress.
\- Further Study: HKUST(GZ) opens applications for the class of 2026 before May 10th. The notice arrangement is unreasonable. I am worried about the application time for grade 2023 and have to prioritize domestic postgraduate recommendation as a backup.
&\#x20;
4. Reflection
&\#x20;
\- Laziness has become a deep-rooted problem. I always procrastinate and rush to finish tasks at the last minute with extremely low time utilization.
\- I have no systematic thinking, fail to make long-term plans, and rely too much on temporary enthusiasm.
\- My self-control is poor. I indulge myself when I am slack and stay up late blindly when I want to make progress.
&\#x20;
5. Next Steps
&\#x20;
\- Get rid of the slack attitude, establish a systematic daily schedule and stick to stable execution.
\- Make full use of free time, reduce invalid rest and improve the efficiency of time arrangement.
\- Maintain a regular schedule, distribute daily tasks evenly in the daytime, and avoid impulsive late-night overtime.
\- Settle down, take practical actions, and cultivate lasting perseverance and self-discipline.
