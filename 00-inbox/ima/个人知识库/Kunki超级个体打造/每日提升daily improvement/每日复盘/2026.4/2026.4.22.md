Daily Review – April 23
&\#x20;
Core State
&\#x20;
Today was extremely frustrating and unlucky for me. I felt upset all day long. I was urged by my tutor to revise an old paper and spent plenty of time on trivial tasks. I tried to improve my personal image and tidy up my room, but unexpected troubles came one after another in the evening.
&\#x20;
I suffered financial loss because the password card was used abnormally for no reason, and I had to compensate my partner at my own cost. What was worse, I accidentally threw away my expensive medicine in a hurry and had to buy it again, which made me lose money twice in one day. I was totally exhausted and lost motivation for everything. I also feel anxious about my upcoming trip to Hangzhou because I haven’t prepared my luggage properly.
&\#x20;
1. Mood & Mentality
I was in a low mood all day. The continuous bad accidents completely broke my state. I realized that I have been impetuous and careless recently, leading to frequent silly mistakes. Nevertheless, I still believe that interpersonal trust is more important than small profits, and I choose to take the loss voluntarily rather than hurt the relationship with my partner.
2. Daily Schedule
I attended classes in the morning and had lunch with my classmates at noon. After returning to the dormitory, I relaxed for a while and was then arranged to modify the format of a paper written one year ago. In the afternoon, I learned about personal image management and sorted out my messy room. I also attended an ideological and political lecture in the evening with flexible time arrangement.
3. Business Incident & Reflection
I provided my partner with a card key at the cost price without making any profit. Strangely, the password had already been used by someone else one hour earlier, while neither side leaked it. The root cause was that I left the key unused for too long. In order to maintain trust, I refunded the full money and bore the loss myself. I regard this experience as a valuable lesson and a long-term investment in interpersonal relationships.
4. Careless Mistake
I threw away my valuable medicine by mistake when I hurried out. It was impossible to get it back from the dirty trash can. I had to buy the same medicine again and spent extra money. Although the nurse comforted me with a free facial mask, I still deeply realized that being impetuous will only lead to unnecessary losses.
5. Plan & Expectation
I need to pack my luggage for Hangzhou as soon as possible and decide whether to buy a small suitcase. I hope I can adjust my negative state quickly, get rid of carelessness and impatience, and regain my motivation in the following days.
