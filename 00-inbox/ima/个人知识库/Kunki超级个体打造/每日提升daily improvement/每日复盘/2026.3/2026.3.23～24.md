&\#x20;
今日复盘
&\#x20;

&\#x20;
1. 心态与自我认知
&\#x20;
\- 感觉距离真正的AI创业者差距很大，无论是同龄人还是前辈，都显得不成熟、功底浅；
\- 兼顾学业与创业，觉得自身竞争力不足，前几天情绪低落；
\- 明白有差距才有提升空间，仍有大量事情要做，未完全放弃。
&\#x20;
2. AI行业与自我策略
&\#x20;
\- AI产品爆炸式更新，信息过载，不知道该不该持续体验新工具；
\- 决定不被热点带着走，稳住基本盘最重要；
\- 核心是打磨自己的业务逻辑、工作流、业务场景、语料库；
\- 产品会不断迭代，没必要频繁更换工具、盲目跟风；
\- 开始思考：时间精力有限，如何在AI圈做出差异化、实现盈利。

&\#x20;

\- 早已不关注这类内容，却必须花时间收尾，觉得无奈、倒霉；
\- 承担压力较多，但接受“痛苦是人生常态”，作为成年人必须扛住。
&\#x20;
4. 精神支撑
&\#x20;
\- 极度依赖Leon的视频与播客，提供持续精神力量；
\- 若无思想支撑，可能早已放弃，十分感恩这份支撑。
&\#x20;
5. 两天行为总结
&\#x20;
\- 状态偏摆烂，有效产出不多；
\- 尝试多款AI工具，效果均不理想；
\- 最终回归清醒：专注打磨自身核心体系，不被产品迭代牵着走。

Daily Review (March 23–24)
&\#x20;

I also had to prepare for two project defenses which were started a year ago, bringing me heavy pressure. Supported by Leon’s videos and podcasts, I didn’t give up and began to think about my differentiation and profitability.

1. Mentality & Self-awareness
&\#x20;
\- I felt immature and incompetent compared with other AI entrepreneurs, whether peers or experienced seniors.
\- Balancing study and entrepreneurship made me feel uncompetitive and depressed.
\- I recognized that gaps mean room for improvement, so I didn’t give up completely.

&\#x20;
\- The rapid update of AI tools caused information overload, making me unsure whether to keep trying new ones.
\- I decided not to follow trends blindly, but to stabilize my basic framework.
\- The core lies in my business logic, workflow, application scenarios and corpus.
\- Constant product updates do not mean frequent tool changes.
\- I began to think about how to build differentiation and achieve profitability with limited time and energy.

&\#x20;
\- I had to prepare for two project defenses for programs started a year ago.
\- I am no longer interested in such work but have to finish it, feeling helpless.

&\#x20;

&\#x20;
