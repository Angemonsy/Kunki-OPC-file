&\#x20;

2. 答辩情况：未做极致充分准备，仅背诵答辩稿、梳理项目基础介绍（小白易懂版本），全程靠临场发挥；答辩结束后获老师表扬，称准备充分、表现出色，自身觉得发挥平稳。
3. 项目感悟：意识到该AI低代码项目对跨保数字经济专业助力极大，且项目有实际成果产出，决定后续深入吃透、细化研究。
4. 晚间安排：简单准备次日校级大创答辩，零散做了些辅助工作，探索效果一般，无实质性突破。
&\#x20;
3月26日 核心事项
&\#x20;
1. 上午就医：前往医院皮肤科看诊，医生建议痘坑问题通过激光点阵改善；因急于返校，直接缴纳单次治疗费用（2000余元）。
2. 下午校级答辩：返校后参与校级大创答辩，自身表现一般，PPT为简洁版本，内容偏感受感悟，因对项目陌生、稿子不熟且紧张，分享逻辑稍乱，但顺利完成陈述；发现老师更关注结项书资料，而非PPT内容；因项目有学姐SCI一区论文加持，老师未做额外提问，直接通过，全程轻松过关，此前因老师刁难其他项目的紧张感彻底消除。
3. 晚间放松：健身练肩，训练强度大、耗时久，身体疲惫；独自吃自助餐大餐犒劳自己；回家后浏览小红书了解激光点阵护理流程，发觉护理流程繁琐。
4. 核心决策：因现阶段需兼顾保研准备与AI商业项目，时间、精力极度紧张；且独居在校，护理条件远不如家中，担心护理不当导致皮肤问题加重、得不偿失，并非惧怕疼痛，而是权衡后决定暂缓项目，回家后再做激光点阵，目前皮肤状态尚可，无需急于当下优化。
&\#x20;
总结与反思
&\#x20;
1. 两场答辩均顺利过关，省级项目的价值逐渐凸显，成为保研的重要加分项，后续需重点深挖；
2. 学会权衡优先级，现阶段以保研、AI商业为核心，非紧急事项适当延后，避免分散精力；
3. 劳逸结合的节奏尚可，高压完成任务后及时放松，利于调整状态，后续继续保持。

版本3：英文流畅口语版（English Casual）

Let me recap what I did on March 25th and 26th.

On March 25th, I had classes in the morning. In the afternoon, I went to the North Campus with my classmate from the Information School to defend our provincial AI low-code innovation project, and we successfully concluded it. I didn't prepare that much—just memorized the script and went over the basic project info that beginners can understand, then I just improvised on the spot. Surprisingly, the teachers praised me for my good performance and said I was well-prepared, but I thought it was just okay.

I realized this AI low-code project is really helpful for my cross-major postgraduate recommendation to Digital Economy, and it actually has some achievements. So I plan to dig deeper into it later. In the evening, I did a little preparation for the university-level project defense the next day, but it didn't go that well, just some random stuff.

March 26th, I went to the hospital to see a dermatologist in the morning. The doctor said laser treatment is the best way to fix my acne pits. I was in a hurry to get back to school, so I paid over 2,000 yuan for one treatment right away.

In the afternoon, I had the university-level project defense. I didn't do that great—my PPT was simple, I just talked about my feelings, and I was nervous and unfamiliar with the project and the script, so my sharing was a bit messy. But I noticed teachers didn't really listen to the PPT, they just checked our conclusion materials carefully. Luckily, our project has a senior's SCI Zone 1 thesis, so the teachers didn't ask me any tough questions and let me go directly. I was so relieved, because I was scared to death by their harsh questions to other students before.

In the evening, I worked out my shoulders at the gym for a long time, and I was super tired. Then I had a big buffet dinner by myself to treat myself. After getting home, I browsed Xiaohongshu to learn about laser aftercare, and found it's really troublesome.

Now I'm having second thoughts about the laser treatment. I'm not scared of pain, but I'm super busy with postgraduate recommendation and my AI business right now, time and energy are really tight. And I live alone off campus, I can't take care of my skin as well as I do at home. I'm worried if I don't nurse it well, my skin will get worse. My current skin is not that bad, I just want to make it better. So I think I'll put it off and do the laser treatment when I go back home.

Overall, these two days were busy but the defenses went well, and I figured out my priority for the next period.
