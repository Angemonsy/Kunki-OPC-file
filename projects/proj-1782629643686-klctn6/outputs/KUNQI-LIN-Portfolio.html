01 Academic ResearchPortfolio 02 / 06
From economic question to published paper

Critical minerals, trade networks, and new energy vehicles.

This project is the clearest evidence of my research discipline: define a real sustainability problem, translate it into measurable variables, and test the mechanism with data.

Publication evidence
How trading patterns of critical minerals affects the NEV industry? A complex network and multiple regression analysis approach

Energy for Sustainable Development, Volume 85, 2025, Article 101655
DOI: 10.1016/j.esd.2025.101655
Role: Co-first Author, student contributor

Method visual
QuestionDo global critical mineral trade positions affect NEV industry development?
ApproachConstructed a 2013-2023 trade network and tested mechanisms with econometric models.
FindingTrade centrality promotes NEV industry growth, with technological progress as a full mediator.
My contribution
  • Participated in literature review, hypothesis development, variable design, empirical testing, and manuscript revision.
  • Worked with global trade data and translated an industrial problem into network indicators.
  • Built confidence in cross-domain research across trade, sustainability, and industrial innovation.
Capability signal
