PAGE 2: PROJECT 1 - SCIENTIFIC PUBLICATION

How trading patterns of critical minerals affects the NEV industry? A complex network and multiple regression analysis approach

Authors: Qing Guo, Kunqi Lin (Student First Author)
Publication: Energy for Sustainable Development | Volume 85, 2025, 101655 | ISSN 0973-0826 | SCI Region 2, IF = 4.4 | Published 2025
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.esd.2025.101655
Role: Core Contributor & Student First Author

Project Background

With the global push for carbon neutrality, new energy vehicles (NEVs) have become a critical focus for energy transition policy. However, there remains limited empirical research on how critical minerals trade patterns affect NEV industry development.

My Contributions

  • Full Research Process: Led from scratch: literature review, hypothesis development, variable and indicator system design
  • Data & Analysis: Collected and processed 10 years of global trade data, constructed global critical mineral trade network (2013-2023)
  • Discovery: Identified that technological progress fully mediates the relationship between trade patterns and NEV industry growth
  • Writing: Wrote the complete English manuscript and revised based on reviewer comments

Key Findings

The global critical minerals trade network exhibits "small-world" characteristics with expanding scale and structural stability. Trade network centrality significantly promotes NEV industry development through technological progress.
