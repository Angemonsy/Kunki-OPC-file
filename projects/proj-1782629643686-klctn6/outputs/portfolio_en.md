## PAGE 2: PROJECT 1 - SCIENTIFIC PUBLICATION

### **How trading patterns of critical minerals affects the NEV industry? A complex network and multiple regression analysis approach**  
**Publication**: *Energy for Sustainable Development* | Volume 85, 2025, 101655 | **SCI Region 2**, IF = 4.4 | Published 2025  
**DOI**: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.esd.2025.101655  
**Role**: **Student First Author** (Core Contributor)

### Project Background
With the global push for carbon neutrality, new energy vehicles (NEVs) have become a critical focus for energy transition policy. However, there remains limited empirical research on how critical minerals trade patterns affect NEV industry development.

### My Contributions
- Led **full research process** from scratch: literature review, hypothesis development, variable and indicator system design  
- Collected and processed 10 years of global trade data, constructed trade network metrics including trade intensity and centrality  
- Applied **dynamic panel System GMM** for empirical testing, completed heterogeneity and robustness analysis  
- Identified and verified the "complete mediation mechanism of technological progress"  
- Wrote the complete English manuscript and revised based on reviewer comments

### Key Findings
- Constructed global critical mineral trade network (2013-2023), found the network exhibits "small-world" characteristics with expanding scale and structural stability  
- Identified that trade network centrality significantly promotes NEV industry development through technological progress  
- Provided policy recommendations for countries seeking to develop sustainable automotive industries
