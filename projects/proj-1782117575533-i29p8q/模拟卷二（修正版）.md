**Passage 2: Income Distribution in China**

China is working to improve its income distribution system. Currently, there are still gaps in income between urban and rural areas and between different regions. The government's goal is to build a system where primary, secondary, and tertiary distribution are coordinated and complement each other.

One important objective is to raise the share of personal income in national income distribution. The government also wants to increase the proportion of labor remuneration in primary distribution. Multiple channels are being used to increase the property income of both urban and rural residents. Redistribution is being strengthened through taxation, social security, and transfer payments. The long-term goal is to expand the middle-income group and form an olive-shaped distribution structure that is more stable and sustainable.

**Question 4:**
1. **Free trade area:** Free movement of goods and services among member countries.
2. **Customs union:** Free trade area plus common external tariff against non-members.
3. **Common market:** Customs union plus free movement of production factors (labor and capital).
4. **Economic union:** Common market plus common economic policies.
