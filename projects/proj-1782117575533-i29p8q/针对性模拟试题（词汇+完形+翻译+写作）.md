**Question 4:**
1. **Free trade area:** Members can trade goods and services freely with each other, no internal trade barriers.
2. **Customs union:** Free trade area plus a common external tariff against non-members.
3. **Common market:** Customs union plus free movement of labor and capital (production factors).
4. **Economic union:** Common market plus common economic policies.

### 参考范文：

# Changes in China's Industrial Structure (1)
---
## Introduction

This report analyzes changes in the share of different industries in China's GDP between 2015 and 2025. The data covers primary, secondary and tertiary industries. The goal is to show how China's economic structure has changed over this period.

## Analysis

From the table we can see clear trends. The share of primary industry in GDP has gradually decreased from 8.8% in 2015 to 6.5% in 2025. Similarly, the share of secondary industry has also fallen from 40.9% to 33.5% over the same period.

In contrast, the share of tertiary industry (service sector) has increased steadily from 50.3% in 2015 to 60% in 2025. This means that the service sector now accounts for more than half of China's GDP.

## Conclusion

Overall, the data shows that China's economy is shifting gradually from manufacturing and agriculture towards services. This is a natural trend of economic development and reflects the growth of consumer demand and the digital economy in China.
