Income distribution has been an important policy priority for China in recent years. The government aims to increase personal incomes in step with economic growth, and raise labor remuneration in line with productivity increases. Currently, there are still wide gaps in income distribution between urban and rural areas and between different regions.

To address this issue, China is working to improve the income distribution system. The goal is to build an institutional framework where primary, secondary, and tertiary distribution work together in a coordinated way. One key objective is to raise the share of personal income in national income distribution and give more weight to labor remuneration in primary distribution. Policies are also being implemented to increase property income for both urban and rural residents through multiple channels, and to strengthen redistribution through taxation, social security, and transfer payments. The long-term goal is to expand the middle-income group and form an oval-shaped (olive) distribution structure.

**Passage 2**

International trade can be classified into different types according to various criteria. The most basic distinction is between visible trade (trade in goods) and invisible trade (trade in services). Visible trade involves the exchange of physical, tangible goods that can be seen and touched. Invisible trade involves the exchange of services such as banking, tourism, transportation, and intellectual property.

Another important distinction is between bilateral trade and multilateral trade. Bilateral trade is the exchange of goods and services between just two countries, usually based on negotiated agreements. Multilateral trade involves trade among multiple countries, often under the framework of international agreements like those established by the World Trade Organization.

Processing trade is another common type of trade that is very important for China. In processing trade, domestic firms import raw materials, parts, and components from other countries, process or assemble them into finished products, and then export the finished products. There are two main types: processing with imported materials (import-and-assembly) and processing with supplied materials (pure assembly).
