### 🔹 Unit 2 Section 1 核心词汇（Types of International Trade）

#### 主要贸易类型

| 英文 | 中文释义 |
|------|----------|
| **export** | 出口：将国内商品卖到国外 |
| **import** | 进口：将国外商品带入国内 |
| **transit trade** | 转口贸易：货物经第三国转运，所有权不转移给第三国 |
| **entrepot trade** | 转口贸易/保税仓库贸易：进口后增值再出口，免关税 |
| **bilateral trade** | 双边贸易：两国之间的贸易 |
| **multilateral trade** | 多边贸易：多国之间的贸易 |
| **visible trade / goods trade** | 有形贸易/货物贸易：实体商品贸易 |
| **invisible trade / service trade** | 无形贸易/服务贸易：服务等无形商品贸易 |
| **processing trade** | 加工贸易：进口原材料，加工后出口成品 |
| - processing with imported materials | - 进料加工 |
| - processing with supplied materials | - 来料加工 |
| **compensation trade** | 补偿贸易：用产品抵偿设备技术价款 |
| **countertrade** | 对等贸易：用商品交换商品而非货币 |
| **intra-industry trade** | 产业内贸易：两国交换同一产业的相似产品 |
| **inter-industry trade** | 产业间贸易：一国出口的产品所属部门不同于进口产品 |

#### 名词辨析（填空题考点）

| 英文 | 释义区别 |
|------|----------|
| **goods** (pl.) | 广义的有形物品，包括原材料、成品 |
| **merchandise** [U] | 消费品，零售领域常用 |
| **commodity** [C] | 大宗商品，原材料/农产品（黄金、石油、小麦） |
| **product** [C] | 产品：生产出来的任何物品（有形+无形） |
| **cargo** | 正在被运输的货物 |
| **freight** | 被运输的货物；运费 |

#### WTO基本原则

| 原则 | 含义 |
|------|------|
| **Most-favoured-nation (MFN)** | 最惠国待遇：对所有WTO成员平等对待，给一国优惠必须给所有成员 |
| **National treatment** | 国民待遇：外国商品进入市场后，与国产商品平等对待 |

#### 区域经济一体化四个层次

| 层次 | 特点 |
|------|------|
| **Free trade area** | 自由贸易区：商品服务自由流动 |
| **Customs union** | 关税同盟：自由贸易区 + 共同对外关税 |
| **Common market** | 共同市场：关税同盟 + 生产要素自由流动 |
| **Economic union** | 经济联盟：共同市场 + 共同经济政策 |

### 🔹 Unit 2 Section 2 贸易术语翻译高频考点

#### 询盘常用表达

| 中文 | 英文 |
|------|------|
| 我们对...感兴趣 | We are interested in ... |
| 请报...最低价格 | Please quote us your lowest price for ... |
| 请给我们报...价 | Please offer ... / make an offer for ... |

#### 实盘 (Firm Offer) 常用句型
```
We offer subject to your reply reaching here,

make you the following offer,

The offer is/remains valid/firm/open for 20 days / until June 30.
```
有效期表达：`within two weeks` / `before June 30` / `not later than June 30`

#### 虚盘 (Non-firm Offer) 常用句型
```
We offer subject to

We make the following offer without engagement/obligation.
This offer is without engagement,
```
常见条件：`subject to our final confirmation`（以我方最后确认为准）/ `subject to goods being unsold`（以货物未售出为准）

#### 价格条款写法示例
- EUR100 per carton **FOB Guangzhou** Incoterms 2020
- USD100 per dozen **CIFC2 London** （CIF伦敦含2%佣金）

#### 包装常用表达
- packed in ... 用...包装
- packed in cartons of ... each 每箱装...
- **bulk cargo / in bulk** 散装货 / 散装
- **nude cargo / in nude** 裸装货 / 裸装

#### 装运常用表达
- Shipment during June to August 2026,
- with partial shipment and transshipment allowed 允许分批装运和转运
- **FCL** Full Container Load 整箱货

#### 保险常用表达
- **FPA (Free From Particular Average)** 平安险
- **WA/WPA (With Average)** 水渍险
- **All Risks** 一切险
- Insurance: To be covered by the buyer (FOB/CFR术语下)

## 📌 四、写作题（30分，图表分析报告）
### 评分规则（老师明确说）
- 严格审题，不遗漏要求
- **必须合理分段**，全篇不分段直接判 **10分以下**
- 文章具备**正式报告格式与篇章结构**
- 语言要用上本学期经贸英语所学知识点
- 达标就可以 **24分起评**

### 考试预测：图表数据分析报告（课堂练过这个类型）
一般给你一个表格，有几年的数据，让你分析变化趋势。

### 正式报告标准结构（三段式）

```
1. Introduction / Introduction
   - 一句话介绍报告主题，说明表格是什么数据

2. Body / Analysis
   - 分析主要趋势，总体变化
   - 分析具体数据变化（增长/下降/占比变化）
   - 可以分两段：一段说总体，一段说细节

3. Conclusion / Conclusion / Recommendations
   - 总结趋势，简单评论或展望
```

### 写作常用经贸词汇短语（按主题分类）

| 主题 | 常用表达 |
|------|----------|
| **经济增长** | GDP growth, annual growth rate, nominal GDP, real GDP, per capita GDP, maintain steady growth |
| **产业结构** | primary/secondary/tertiary industry, advanced manufacturing, digital transformation, service sector, industrial structure |
| **区域发展** | regional development, coordinated development, city cluster, Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei, Yangtze River Delta, Greater Bay Area |
| **收入分配** | income growth, disposable income, income gap, middle-income group, urban and rural |
| **国际贸易** | trade volume, export, import, trading partner, total trade, bilateral trade |
| **数字化** | digital economy, digital transformation, core digital industries, share of GDP |

### 描述变化常用句型

- **增长**：`... increased from ... in ... to ... in ...` / `... saw a steady growth from ... to ...` / `the percentage of ... rose to ...`
- **下降**：`... decreased from ... to ...` / `the share of ... fell gradually from ... to ...`
- **保持稳定**：`... remained relatively stable at ...` / `the percentage stayed at about ...`
- **总结趋势**：`Overall, we can see that ...` / `From the data we can conclude that ...`

### 范文框架示例（以人均可支配收入题为例）

**Introduction:**
> This report analyzes the changes in per capita disposable income in China between 2015 and 2025. The data covers national average, urban areas and rural areas.

**Body:**
> Overall, per capita disposable income increased steadily for all groups over the ten-year period. The national average rose from 21,966 yuan in 2015 to 42,800 yuan in 2025.
>
> Both urban and rural incomes saw significant growth. Urban income increased from 31,195 yuan to 56,200 yuan, while rural income went up from 11,422 yuan to 24,300 yuan. Although the absolute gap between urban and rural remains, both groups are becoming better off.

**Conclusion:**
> In conclusion, Chinese residents have seen considerable income growth over the past decade. This shows that China's economic development has brought real benefits to ordinary people in both urban and rural areas.
